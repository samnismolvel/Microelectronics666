Sumary

The idea of ​​producing small, energy-efficient, multifunctional devices has become an end-user demand. Progress has been made. From enormous console radios with multiple bulbs to cell phones with internet, television, and radio services; the distance traveled is enormous, but the demand is greater. In this race, expectations may prevail over suppliers' capabilities, in a kind of investment in the technological race. It's no longer about what science and technology offer, but about what the consumer demands. Significant advances have been made, but the possibilities are reaching their limits, and options are dwindling as demand increases.
