About

Bachelor student

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering at the Madero Institute of Technology. He participated in the National Institute of Astrophysics, Optics and Electronics (INAOE) summer research programs from 2022 to 2026, developing his skills in various areas of digital and analog electronics. This led him to conduct several research projects focused on FGMOS for the implementation of new logic circuits. He is currently completing his Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering at the Madero Institute of Technology and plans to pursue a Master's degree and ultimately a Doctorate.

Areas of Research

  • Performs integrated circuit design (digital, analog and layout).
  • Manipulates programs for designs such as Cadence and Spice.
  • Develops web page design projects using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • HMI interface design and signal acquisition based on Python
  • NVIDIA JETSON: From Configuration to Artificial Intelligence
  • Introduction to Verilog: Intel and AMD
